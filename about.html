About Better Call Us

Our mission is to save local independent hospitality from closing its doors.

Our Mission

To provide hands-on, actionable, and performance-based support that transforms struggling independent cafés and restaurants into highly profitable, smoothly running local favorites.

Our Vision

A thriving local high street in Edinburgh and Glasgow where no independent food business has to close due to a lack of operational or financial expertise.

Who We Help

We specifically focus on independent, small-scale cafés and restaurants in Edinburgh and Glasgow.

High Competition

The rise of chain stores makes it hard for independents to stand out.

Rising Costs

Energy bills and supply chain costs are destroying profit margins.

Lack of Expertise

Many owners are great cooks, but struggle with spreadsheets and spreadsheets.

Meet Our Ideal Clients

Sarah, 34

Independent Café Owner in Edinburgh

The Struggle: Sarah loves coffee and baking, but she's working 80-hour weeks. Her staff turnover is high, and at the end of the month, she barely takes a salary home.

What she needs: Someone to help her systematize operations, hire reliable staff, and find ways to cut costs without reducing quality.

Ahmed, 45

Family Restaurant Owner in Glasgow

The Struggle: Ahmed's family restaurant has been struggling since the 2020 pandemic. Footfall is down, and rent has increased. He's bleeding cash.

What he needs: A strong marketing strategy to bring back locals and a menu audit to maximize margin on his best-selling dishes.
