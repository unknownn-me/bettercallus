Case Study 1: The "70-Hour Week" Café

Edinburgh

The Problem

An independent coffee shop in central Edinburgh generating decent revenue but bleeding cash through high waste, poor staff scheduling, and an over-complicated menu. The owner was working 70 hours a week and drawing a salary below minimum wage.

Our Implementation

  • Menu Audit: Scrapped 20% of low-margin menu items, re-priced core goods to absorb recent supplier hikes automatically.
  • Waste Tracking: Implemented a strict daily waste ledger reducing food spoilage from 8% to 2%.
  • Scheduling: Adjusted rotas to match actual hourly footfall data, reducing labour cost without sacrificing customer service times.

Case Study 2: The Negative-Margin Restaurant

Glasgow

Operations Dashboard Preview

Gross Profit Margin ↑ 71%
Supply Chain Cost ↓ 14%
Staff Turnover Rate ↓ 5%

The Turnaround Strategy

A family-owned restaurant running at a net loss for 6 consecutive months. Energy bills had doubled and ingredient costs had surged. They were terrified of raising prices because they feared losing their regulars.

We implemented our Strategic Pricing Model. Instead of blanket price hikes, we adjusted the pricing of high-volume items slightly while significantly reducing the portion sizes of high-cost proteins (without affecting the perceived value of the dish). This instantly recovered 12% in gross profit margin without a single customer complaint.
