Marketing Strategy

Our approach to finding, converting, and retaining clients.

STP Model

S

Segmentation

We segment the market structurally by geographic density (Edinburgh & Glasgow urban centres) and demographically. Specifically, we isolate independent hospitality businesses (cafés, bistros, and small restaurants) generating an annual turnover between £200,000 and £500,000. Psychographically, we filter for owners demonstrating high stress due to rapid inflation, wage increases, and post-pandemic economic pressures. This allows for highly localized, cost-effective resource allocation.

T

Targeting

Our primary targeting strategy focuses heavily on the exhausted owner-operator demographic. These individuals typically work 60+ hour workweeks acting "in" the business rather than "on" it, leaving them blind to chronic operational inefficiencies. By directing focused digital marketing towards their specific pain points—such as staff retention, shrinking net margins, and high food wastage—we bypass generic business advice seekers. We utilize a concentrated, niche marketing approach rather than a differentiated mass-market strategy.

P

Positioning

We leverage a unique "hands-on" positioning paradigm. In an industry saturated with expensive, theoretical corporate consultancies, Better Call Us physically embeds itself within the client's operation. We lead from the front, physically working the floor to gain immediate staff buy-in. Our risk-averse pricing matrix, hinging heavily on a backend profit-share model (15% of newly generated net profit), creates an immediate competitive advantage and builds unquestionable trust.

AIDA Conversion Funnel

Awareness (Cognitive Stage)

We generate primary top-of-funnel awareness through pain-point specific Instagram Reels and B2B LinkedIn content. Content directly addresses common operational crises (e.g., "Why your food costs are slowly killing your café"). By utilizing localized SEO (search term: "hospitality consultant Edinburgh") and socially engineered viral content, we penetrate the noise to capture the initial attention of stressed operators.

Interest (Affective Stage)

Once captured, attention is transformed into interest by routing prospects to our heavily optimized website. Here, they encounter free, actionable value—such as our Market Research reports and operational calculators. By illustrating that we truly understand the micro-economics of the Scottish hospitality scene, we nurture curiosity without immediate aggressive selling.

Desire (Affective Stage)

Desire is stimulated through empirical proof. Our dedicated 'Case Studies' portfolio highlights dramatic transformational metrics (e.g., moving a venue from a £2,000 monthly deficit to £4,000 net profit). By clearly articulating our unique selling proposition (USP)—a performance-based profit-share model rather than high upfront fixed fees—we lower perceived risk and elevate the emotional and financial necessity of our service.

Action (Behavioral Stage)

The final behavioral trigger is a frictionless, low-commitment Call To Action (CTA). We eliminate complex onboarding barriers by offering a strictly no-obligation "Free 30-Minute Discovery Call." Strategically placed UX elements, such as highly visible primary buttons and simplified form validations, ensure the conversion boundary remains minimal.

SMART Goals (Year 1)

  • Specific (S): Successfully onboard 15 independent hospitality venues (specifically cafés, bistros, or small footprint restaurants) situated within the central belt of Scotland (Edinburgh and greater Glasgow regions) to our full Phase-2 monthly retainer and Phase-3 profit-share contracts.
  • Measurable (M): Generate a stabilized Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) base of £10,500 from retainer fees and projected profit shares by the end of the 12th operational month, to be tracked against a trailing quarterly average using CRM financial dashboards.
  • Achievable (A): Presuming an industry-standard B2B conversion rate of 10% from digital marketing leads to qualified discovery calls, securing 1.25 new client contracts per month is completely feasible. This builds in a theoretical 15% churn allowance for businesses failing despite interventions.
  • Relevant (R): This core objective directly aligns with the broader organizational mission statement: salvaging the independent Scottish hospitality sector from post-pandemic economic collapse while building localized market authority.
  • Time-bound (T): The objective operates on a strict 12-month timeline originating from the date of the primary marketing campaign launch, punctuated by quarterly benchmark audits to ensure pacing integrity.
