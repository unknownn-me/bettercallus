Marketing Strategy

Our approach to finding, converting, and retaining clients.

STP Model

S

Segmentation

We segment the market geographically (Edinburgh & Glasgow) and demographically (Independent food businesses turning over £200k - £500k).

T

Targeting

We target owner-operators who are working 60+ hours a week but seeing shrinking profit margins due to operational inefficiencies.

P

Positioning

We position ourselves as the affordable, fully hands-on alternative to massive corporate consultancy firms. We do the work with them.

AIDA Conversion Funnel

Awareness

Pain-point focused Instagram Reels targeting local restaurant owners, combined with B2B LinkedIn posts showing basic operational tips.

Interest

Directing traffic to the Better Call Us website, specifically the "Services" and "Research" pages highlighting industry struggles.

Desire

Showcasing detailed case studies of previous cafés saved from closure, demonstrating our low-risk profit-share model.

Action

Frictionless booking of a free 30-minute consultation call via our clean, optimized booking page.

SMART Goals (Year 1)

  • Specific: Onboard 15 independent cafés or restaurants in the central belt of Scotland to full retaining contracts.
  • Measurable: Achieve £10,500 MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) by month 12.
  • Achievable: At 2 onboardings a month, accounting for churn, this requires a realistic lead conversion rate of 10%.
  • Relevant: Focuses purely on our core demographic to establish highly specialized local authority.
  • Time-bound: Goals to be met exactly 12 months from launch.

Our Revenue Model

Phase 1: The Initial Audit

£495
