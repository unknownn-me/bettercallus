Tools Used

ChatGPT

ChatGPT functioned as our primary research and structural assistant. It was instrumental in brainstorming core marketing approaches, structuring complex theoretical frameworks (such as STP and AIDA), and drafting academic-grade business copy. It significantly accelerated ideation and refined our professional tone.

Canva

Canva powered our entire visual identity and UX wireframing process. We utilized it heavily to construct high-fidelity UI elements, curate our vector illustration style, and design the localized Instagram marketing assets displayed in our case studies. It ensured a premium, consistent brand aesthetic.

VS Code

Visual Studio Code (VS Code) served as our core Integrated Development Environment (IDE). Through VS Code, we engineered the raw HTML5 structure, layered modular CSS3 for our responsive design system, and implemented strict accessibility (a11y) features to meet top-tier university marking standards.
