Privacy Policy & Legal

Transparency on data collection, GDPR compliance, and our Terms of Service.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Better Call Us ("we", "our", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data holding strict compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide our consultancy services effectively. When you use our booking forms or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, business name.
  • Contact Data: Email address, telephone number, business address.
  • Financial Data: High-level financial positioning provided voluntarily during the audit onboarding.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, time zone setting (managed securely via cookies).

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data strictly to:

  • Schedule consultations and physically visit your venue.
  • Draft secure, confidential operational audits.
  • Manage our client relationship, including billing and profit-share tracking.

We absolutely never sell your data to third parties or marketing aggregate platforms.

4. Data Security

We have put in place robust security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. All operational and financial reports generated during our consultancy are protected with encryption and restricted solely to the consultancy pair managing your account.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses necessary cookies to ensure basic functionality. We may also use analytical scripts (such as Google Analytics) to understand site traffic patterns, which helps us position our marketing strategies. You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when websites set or access cookies.

6. Your Legal Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to request access, correction, erasure, or restricted processing. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@bettercallus.co.uk.

Terms of Service Draft

By engaging with Better Call Us, you agree to the foundational terms of our service:

  • The 15% profit-share arrangement applies explicitly to the marginal net profit increase calculated against the 6-month baseline preceding our audit.
  • Both parties reserve the right to terminate the consultancy arrangement with a standard 30-day notice period.
  • All strategic documentation, training manuals, and digital content generated by Better Call Us for your venue remains your property upon full payment of the monthly retainer.
